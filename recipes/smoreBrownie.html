Smores Brownies

Description

Get the classic campfire flavors of s'mores in a pan of rich, fudgy brownies. Home cooks like the flexible topping options, which let them use chocolate chips or cereal. Allrecipes member Feed my hungry family says, "...this is quick, easy and loved by all."

Ingredients

    For the brownies:
  • 1 (16 ounce) package fudge brownie mix (such as Betty Crocker®)
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons water
    • For the smores:
  • 6 graham crackers
  • 1 ½ cups miniature marshmallows
  • 8 (1.5 ounce) bars milk chocolate, coarsely chopped

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Stir brownie mix, oil, eggs, and water together in a medium bowl until well blended.
  3. Pour batter into the prepared pan. Bake in the preheated oven for 15 minutes.
  4. While the brownies are baking, make the topping: Break graham crackers into 1-inch pieces and place into a bowl.
  5. Add marshmallows and chopped chocolate and toss to combine.
  6. Remove brownies from the oven and sprinkle with topping ingredients.
  7. Return to the oven and continue to bake until a toothpick inserted 2 inches from the side of the pan comes out clean, 7 to 10 more minutes.
  8. Slice, serve, and enjoy!
