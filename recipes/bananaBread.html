Banana Bread

Description

This is a simple and delicious banana bread recipe that's perfect for breakfast or a snack. This one-bowl recipe comes together quickly, using staples like bananas, eggs, sugar, and butter.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups white sugar
  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • 3 bananas, mashed
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ⅓ cup sour milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Lightly grease an 8x4-inch loaf pan.
  2. Combine sugar, butter, bananas, and eggs in a large mixing bowl.
  3. Add in flour, milk, vanilla, baking soda, and salt; beat well. Pour batter into the prepared pan.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of the loaf comes out clean, about 60 minutes.
